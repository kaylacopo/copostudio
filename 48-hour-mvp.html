Questions

FAQ
Can you really build an MVP in 48 hours?

A functional version of your MVP, yes. We agree before the start what that version includes, and it goes live within 48 hours of the confirmed start. The full agreed scope is not finished in 48 hours: after launch come 7 days of priority updates, then continued development.

Will every feature and integration be live in 48 hours?

Not always. What is guaranteed is the agreed MVP version, live and working. Some things are out of my hands in that time, like approvals from other companies, third-party reviews and account setups, so those follow in the updates.

Is it a real, working product?

Yes. People can use the agreed MVP version on desktop or mobile from the first 48 hours, and it grows with every update toward the full agreed scope. It is not a slideshow or a placeholder.

Do I get the full product, or just the 48-hour version?

The full agreed scope, built in stages. The 48-hour version is the first live release. After that you get 7 days of priority updates, then I continue building the rest of the agreed MVP. The final quote depends on complexity.

How long does the full product take?

Usually 1 to 3 months, depending on complexity and whether you also need brand work. The first 7 days after launch are priority, then the rest is built on a standard timeline. You have a live version the whole time, instead of waiting for one big launch at the end.

Will it be on the App Store or Google Play?

No. It runs in the browser on a shareable link. App store releases need a full mobile build and approval process, which is quoted separately.

When can you start?

Express slots are limited and depend on availability. Get in touch with your deadline and I'll tell you the next available start date.

What do I need to have ready?

A clear idea of the product and what you need to show first, your brand assets if you have them, and the agreed payment. The clock only starts once these are in.
